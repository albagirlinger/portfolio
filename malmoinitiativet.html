Malmöinitiativet

A redesign of Malmöinitiativet’s website.

UI Design DX Design

The Task

As part of the Designstudio course, the brief was to redesign Malmöinitiativet - an existing service where residents of Malmö can submit, comment on and vote for ideas about how the city should develop. The goal was to create a modern version of the service, rethinking both its design and functionality from the ground up.

The Process

Working as a group, we began by reviewing the existing service and discussing what we wanted to change, both functionally and visually. From there, we moved into wireframing before developing the high-fidelity design in Figma.

Since the course focused entirely on design, the project stopped at the design stage - the redesign was never built or coded.

Wireframes

The Results

The final result was a redesigned concept for Malmöinitiativet, covering the full flow from browsing and submitting ideas to viewing details and voting. The design focused on making it easier to discover popular initiatives and understand how the voting process works.
